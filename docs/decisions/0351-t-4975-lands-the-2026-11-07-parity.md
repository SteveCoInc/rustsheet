# 0351 — T-4975 lands the 2026-11-07 parity findings and accepts their few baseline regressions

- **Status:** accepted (2026-11-08, T-4975). Owner rulings of 2026-11-07, recorded in T-4975's task file.
- **Context:**
  - T-4975 implements, in one PR, every fix in `docs/research/excel-parity-findings-2026-11-07.md` and its three
    addenda (`-addendum.md`, `-addendum-2.md`, `-addendum-3.md`), plus both audit sections. The owner ruled
    (a) one PR for all of it, overriding directive 4's size and lane rules for this hand-off; (b) the findings are
    trusted as written; (c) a few gated regressions are accepted where the net gain is large, rather than reworked;
    (d) a Gate A point that the new LINEST kernel misses is removed from the gate rather than chased.
  - The combined change fixes well over two thousand known-failing eval cells across gen-m9, ironcalc-calc,
    fixtures and probes, and removes the Gate A known-failure lines the gate itself reports as stale (IRR, CUMIPMT,
    LINEST/LOGEST/TREND/GROWTH, BETA.INV/BETAINV, F.INV, F.INV.RT, CONFIDENCE.T). The exact counts are in T-4975's
    Outcome.
  - A small number of gated cells move by 1–6 ulp the wrong way. They come from four structural changes whose
    gains dominate:
    - findings item 12 (LINEST/LOGEST centered pivoted Householder QR): mostly one-variable fits without a constant
      and GROWTH/TREND cells where EXP amplifies a few ulp of slope (probes growth-modes, logest-modes,
      linest-modes, regression-trend, t4590-growth-overflow, t4765-growth-factor-edges; ironcalc TREND_GROWTH K5).
      For the no-constant fits Excel's slope is the correctly rounded Σxy/Σx², but special-casing it lowers other
      Gate A points, so the precision Excel uses there is not identified;
    - findings item 8 (T.INV small-beta tail): probes t4541-t-inv-2t-tiny-p Calc!K4/K5, 1 ulp;
    - addendum sections A–C and addendum-2 G (BETA.INV/F.INV/T.INV AS 109 stopping points): about twenty
      gen-m9, ironcalc-calc and probes cells at 1–6 ulp (e.g. `T.INV(0.2,5)`, `F.INV(0.975,3,10)`), against 652
      fixed cells in the same families;
    - the visible-parity lock of probes/stat-argument-modes and t3185-m9-statistical-argument-kinds-c rises with
      those cells.
  - Gate A point accuracy/LINEST/s1!Calc!A184 (an intercept formed by cancelling ±4.6e18 terms whose ulp is 1024)
    is 4 ulp of the cancellation away from Excel's 5206016 under the QR kernel. SPEC-0029 forbids listing a
    committed point as a known failure, and no other gate list covers it.
- **Decision:**
  - `rustsheet-eval update-baseline --allow-regressions` runs once in T-4975's PR. It records, and only records,
    the regressed cells above as known failures, and raises the visible-parity lock lines of the cases holding them.
    The baseline diff lists them exactly; reviewers confirm it adds nothing else.
  - accuracy/LINEST/s1!Calc!A184 is removed from the batch's `check` list (its formula and Excel value stay in the
    table, so the point can be re-checked by restoring one line). The batch file is otherwise unchanged.
  - Stale Gate A known-failure lines are removed as the gate instructs (SPEC-0029 step 4).
- **Consequences:**

    Leads recorded for them: summing the Householder reflector's dot product in double-double reproduces A184 in
    simulation (not yet run against Gate A); the t4541 cells sit at p ≈ 2·DBL_MIN.
  - This record does not widen `--allow-regressions` beyond T-4975; later PRs follow quality-gates.md as before.
  - Rollback: revert T-4975's PR.
