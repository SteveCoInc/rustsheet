## Context
T-3980 added a `+%` prefix and refused a minus after `-%` in `parse_percent_or_currency`
(formatter/parse_formatted.rs), and refused a trailing `$` in `parse_typed_number` before every typed fallback.
Unprobed consequences: `+%-1.5`, `+% 1.5`, `+%1e2`, `-% -1.5`, `-%+1.5`, typed `+%1.5`, and typed `3 1/2$` (mixed
fraction with a trailing `$`). Also no cell checks the typed `0.00%` format of `-%1.5` (an ENTERED log in
docs/research/t3980-typed-percent-currency-2026-11-03.md): add a display-text cell or unit test.
Listed in docs/excel-semantics/coercion.md under "Unverified extrapolations".

## Outcome
Probe `probes/t3991-percent-shapes` (excel-graph, 2026-11-04, 21 cells) covers every shape. Excel refuses a second sign after any signed leading percent (`+%-1.5`, `-%+1.5`, `-% -1.5` are #VALUE!); the engine read the first two as -0.015, fixed in `parse_percent_or_currency`. Typed `+%1.5` is 0.015 (`0.00%`), `-%1.5` displays `-1.50%`, `3 1/2$` stays text. coercion.md updated. Eval: new probe 21/21 pass, t3980 probe still 20/20.
