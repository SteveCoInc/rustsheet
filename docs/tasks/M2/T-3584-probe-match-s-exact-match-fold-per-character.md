## Goal
Excel evidence states, per character, which case pairs MATCH's exact match (type 0) treats as equal over the
characters of probes/t3298-case-mapping-sweep, and pins the rule that characters Excel's comparison table lacks
compare equal to one another.

## Context
- probes/t3298-case-mapping-sweep Calc!C1:D48 give, per block of 32 one-to-one case pairs (Data!D upper, Data!E
  lower), the first position MATCH finds for each lower among the block's uppers (C) and the reverse (D). Of 1514
  pairs, 982 match each other both ways, 374 match nothing, and 158 match another position.
- The 158 show a quirk lookup.md now states only as an observation: characters Excel's table seems not to know
  (Unicode additions after about 5.0 in the BMP) equal one another, like characters with no sort weight. For example
  Calc!C17 position 22: the Cherokee small letter U+AB70 matches U+10C7 (position 20), and Calc!C15 position 4:
  U+0529 matches U+0524. Supplementary-plane letters never fold and never match one another (C38 and D38 from position 24, and C39:D48,
  are all 0). Calc!C37 and D37 (the end of Latin Ext-D) are the densest block of such matches.
- Questions for one-cell-per-question probes (build the text with UNICHAR):
  - Does `MATCH(UNICHAR(x),{"",...},0)` or `UNICHAR(x)=""` find a weightless character equal to empty text? Is
    "a"&UNICHAR(x) equal to "a"? Do two different weightless characters equal each other in `=`, EXACT, COUNTIF,
    XMATCH(..,0) and VLOOKUP(..,FALSE)?
  - Which BMP characters are weightless: probe one representative of every block the sweep shows "other", then
    sweep the block edges.
  - Why MATCH equals the small Greek Extended dasia+oxia letters with the capital psili+varia ones (U+1F05 with
    U+1F0A, U+1F15 with U+1F1A, and U+1F25, U+1F35, U+1F45, U+1F65, U+1F85, U+1F95, U+1FA5; Calc!C25:C27), and the
    other unexplained hits lookup.md lists (C35 position 12, C36 position 2, C34 positions 31-32, C8 position 11, C33
    position 32).
  - Whether Cherokee small letters U+AB7B-U+ABBA are weightless like U+AB70-U+AB7A and U+ABBB-U+ABBF.
  - The per-character fold of the "other" pairs in Greek (ϐ ϑ ϕ ϖ ϰ ϱ ϵ µ ι ͅ), Cyrillic Ext-C (U+1C80-U+1C88),
    Latin (ı ſ ǅ ǈ ǋ ǲ ẛ), and Georgian Mtavruli (U+1C90-U+1CBF).
- lookup.md "MATCH, VLOOKUP and HLOOKUP exact-match keys fold case one character to one" holds the current text.

## Outcome
- New probe probes/t3584-match-fold-weightless (3602 cells, excel-graph 2026-11-03), generated by
  docs/research/t3584/gen_case.py and decoded by docs/research/t3584/decode.py into
  docs/research/t3584/weightless-bmp.txt. Four parts: 32 pairs under `=`, EXACT, COUNTIF, MATCH, XMATCH, VLOOKUP and
  HLOOKUP (Calc!A1:H32); the per-character fold of the T-3298 "other" pairs and Georgian Mtavruli (Calc!J1:P85); a
  whole-BMP sweep, one hex digit per code point (Calc!R1:R256); and every character of ten blocks looked up against
  its whole block (Calc!T1:U1232).
- Answers:
  - The weightless set is 2568 BMP code points (381 ranges). It is not a Unicode-version cut; it holds every code
    point unassigned in Unicode 14, the later letters Excel's table lacks, and 51 characters already assigned by Unicode 3.2 (format characters, variation selectors, a few marks).
    Supplementary characters, U+200B and private use are not weightless.
  - Weightless characters equal one another in MATCH, XMATCH(..,0), VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, COUNTIF and `=`, never in
    EXACT, and never equal empty text. `"a"&c` is not `"a"`. Exact MATCH counts them one for one (`"a"&c&"b"` is not
    `"ab"`), while `=`, COUNTIF and XMATCH call `"a"&c&"b"` and `"ab"` equal.
  - U+AB7B-U+ABBA and U+1C80-U+1C88 are weightless. Greek Extended's psili+varia and dasia+oxia letters are one
    class; the other unexplained T-3298 hits are classes of Excel's table (A738-A73B, A764-A767, A76E-A770, Ͷ ͷ ϳ,
    ϑ ϴ, 10D2/10FA) or pairs of weightless characters (A660/A699/A69B, 1C88/2CF2).
- `=`, COUNTIF and XMATCH(..,0) also equate the Greek Extended class, `ϵ` with `ε` and U+1FBE with `ι`, and keep
  Deseret's case pair apart (coercion.md).
- Docs: lookup.md "MATCH, VLOOKUP and HLOOKUP exact-match keys fold case one character to one" now states the
  weightless rule and the class table (VERIFIED); coercion.md has a new section "Weightless characters in `=`,
  COUNTIF and XMATCH (T-3584)".
- Owners: the engine fails 673 of the 3602 cells, all known failures. T-3445 owns the MATCH, VLOOKUP and HLOOKUP
  cells (Calc!E, G, H, K:O, R, T) and no longer depends on this task. New T-3904 (engine, opus) owns the `=`,
  COUNTIF and XMATCH cells (Calc!B:D, F, P), the non-weightless ones (B21, B25, D21, D25, F21, F25, P7, P9) included. It depends on T-3662, which now also probes U+0524 for where a
  weightless character counts in `=`.
