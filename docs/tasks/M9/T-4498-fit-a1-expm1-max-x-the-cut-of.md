## Goal
F.TEST, FTEST, F.INV, F.INV.RT and FINV take the a = 1 tail form Excel takes on each side of the
cut. The cut is placed on T-4497's sweep, and so are the expm1/log1p kernels below it. Every R4
guard of decision 0188 is shown.

## Context
- Needs an oracle sweep: T-4497 supplies the points, its 50-digit math values and the train/hold-out
  split. Do not fit on the 11 cells behind today's tag.
- statistics.md "The a = 1 closed form cancels only from x = 0.1 up (T-0974)": the cut between 4E-4
  and 0.1 is UNVERIFIED and is assumed to be T.DIST.RT's.
- Code: `A1_EXPM1_MAX_X` (incomplete_beta.rs, `FITTED(T-0974, 11 cells, x 1E-11..4E-4 and 0.1)`)
  and `unoriented_shape_one_tail` (fisher.rs). The latter uses libm's `ln_1p`/`exp_m1` below the
  cut, while `shape_one_tails` uses Excel's Kahan expm1 and stored-atanh log1p (T-3170). If both
  places end up with the same kernels, they share one definition (directive 3).
- The oriented closed-form cut `A1_CLOSED_FORM_MAX_Y` (0.9) is a separate fit. Leave it alone.
