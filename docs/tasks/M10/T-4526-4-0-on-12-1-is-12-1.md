## Goal
`TEXT(12.1,"# ?/4|0")` is "12 1/4|00" as in Excel.

## Context
Found by T-4069: probes/t4069-fixed-denominator-tail S!A4 (ours "12      "). With digit placeholders after a fixed denominator Excel picks the fraction as for placeholders of the denominator's width (T-4069), so `?/4|0` picks from denominators up to 9. 0.1 has no convergent there but 0/1, yet Excel prints "1/4" and an extra "0" after the tail. Probe first: `# ?/4|0` on 12.05, 12.1, 12.15, 12.11, 12.12; `# ?/?|0` and `# ?/?` on 12.1; `# ?/4|00` and `# ?/4 x0` on 12.1. Code: formatter/fraction.rs `format_fraction`, `last_convergent`. Semantics: number-formats.md "Digit placeholders after the denominator".
  Owns probes/t4069-fixed-denominator-tail.case.json S!A4.
